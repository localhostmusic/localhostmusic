What it does

The player and the server are one program, so everything below works the same on every device.

Plays without gaps

Albums run track to track with no silence between them, or crossfade if you prefer. Volume is evened out across songs.

Lyrics that follow along

Synced lyrics are fetched when they exist. When they don't, you can time your own by tapping along to the song.

Visualizers

Ten styles that react to the music and take their colours from the album cover.

Playlists and ratings

Right-click a song or album to add it to a playlist. Select several at once. Rate and favourite as you go.

More than music

Podcasts, internet radio and audiobooks live in the same app, each with its own section.

Works with other apps

The server speaks the Subsonic API, so existing Subsonic apps on your phone can connect to it.

Nightcore and slowed

One dial takes a song from slowed with reverb at 0.7× up to 2×, and the whole app changes its look to match.

Listen together

Start a session and other people on your server hear what you play, in sync. Or hand playback from your phone to your computer.

It keeps playing

Start a radio from any song, or let Auto DJ pick what comes next when your queue runs out. There is a sleep timer too.

Yours, and it stays that way

Your library never leaves your devices. The app only goes online for things you can switch off, such as lyrics and artist photos. It is free, with no paid tier, and the source is public.
